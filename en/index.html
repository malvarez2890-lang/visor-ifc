Free online IFC viewer: open BIM models in your browser

IFC Viewer opens .ifc files right in the browser, on desktop, tablet or phone. The file is processed on your own device and never uploaded to a server. No account, no install.

Why use it

Your files never leave your device

The IFC is read and drawn inside the browser. No account, no cloud upload, so confidential models can be reviewed safely.

Works on any device

It uses WebGL, so it opens in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, on tablets and phones too.

Free, nothing to install

It opens from a link. There is no file limit beyond what your device can handle.

What it can do

Views and orientation cube

Isometric, plan, front and side with smooth transitions. A 3D cube jumps to any face, edge or corner and rotates in 90° steps. The plan view cuts the model 1 m above the floor.

Section cuts with fill

Cutting planes on the X, Y and Z axes (Z is height), which can be combined, with black fill on the cut sections and a flip option for each side.

Measurements

Point-to-point distance (with horizontal and vertical components), angles, polyline length and perimeter, and the dimensions and volume of an element's bounding box.

Exploded view

Separates foundations, slabs, structure and roof to show how the model is assembled.

Tree by class and by floor

Browse Project, Site, Building, Storey and element, or by IFC class. Hide or isolate a whole storey in one click and search by name, class or ID.

Element properties

Attributes, property sets (Pset) and quantities (Qto) of the selected element.

Layers, colour and X-ray

Show or hide classes. Colour modes: clay, realistic, original IFC, by class and structural. X-ray to see through the selected element.

Rooms

Labels with the name and area of every room (IfcSpace) in the model, with the roof hidden.

Saved views

Save the camera, cut, layers, colour and isolation, and return to them any time.

Controls your way

Choose what each mouse button does, with Revit-style or Rhino-style presets, and switch between light and dark themes.

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No account, no trial period and no file limit beyond what your device can handle.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. The file is read and drawn in your own browser. It never travels over the network.

Which formats does it open?

.ifc files (STEP format). It does not open .ifczip or .ifcxml yet.

Does it work on phones and tablets?

Yes. On narrow screens the model panel becomes a bottom sheet. Very heavy models may be slow on modest devices.

Which browser do I need?

Any modern browser with WebGL: Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
